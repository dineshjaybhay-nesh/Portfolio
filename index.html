Airtribe Capstone Case Study

Fireflies.ai — From Meeting Notes to Closed Commitments

Designing a closed-loop commitment experience for AI meeting notes. Transforming lost action items into active, tracked, and confirmed resolutions.

Open Full Tab

User Discovery & Primary Data

9
1-on-1 Interviews
32
Survey Respondents
50
Desk Datasets
The Core Gap: "Capture works. Closure doesn't." Action items logged in summaries move into daily chaos without explicit dates or reminders.
North Star Target: Increase 14-Day Commitment Resolution Rate from 28% → 45% (+17 point relative lift).
Stakeholders Covered

Sales / BD Leaders, Account Managers, Operations Managers, Quality Leads, App Developers, and Business Analysts.

Product Solution Architecture
01

FireSpark — Smart Task Creation

Capture + Confirm

Auto-extracts full commitment details directly from transcripts with explicit Owner + Due Date. Enforces human confirmation (Confirm / Edit / Reject) before creating active tasks to prevent AI hallucination errors.

02

FireTrail — Task Dashboard & Context

Track + Visualize

Dedicated workspace with dual views: "My Commitments" (Owner execution) and "Team Commitments" (Manager visibility with RAG health status, blockers, and % completion without micromanagement).

03

FirePulse — Context-Aware Nudges

Remind + Drive Action

Intelligent status check nudges based on due dates and activity signals. Asks "On track / Blocked / Need more time". Automatically stops nudges upon resolution (Completed, Reassigned, or Dropped with reason).
